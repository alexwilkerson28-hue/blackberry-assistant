Account
Welcome to BlackBerry Assistant Pro!
1
Sign Up or Sign In
Create a free account with your name, email, city and password. Next time just sign in with your email and password.
2
Tap the Coloured Tiles
Each tile opens a feature inside the app. Nothing opens a new browser tab — everything stays right here.
■ Green = Alarm & Find Location
■ Blue = Search anything with AI
■ Red = Sports & Tickets
3
Type in the Search Bar
Type any question and press the arrow button or press Enter. The AI will answer it right inside the app with voice.
Try: "What is the weather today?"
4
Use the Mic for Voice Conversations
Tap the circle mic button at the bottom. The blue orb screen appears. Tap the orb to speak. The assistant listens, answers, and speaks back. You can keep talking back and forth!
Say: "What sports games are on today?"
Say: "Tell me a joke"
Say: "What time is it?"
Say: "Find me a restaurant"
5
Sports Changes Every Day
The sports tile automatically shows a different sport every day of the week — NFL on Sundays and Mondays, NBA on Tuesdays and Fridays, MLB on Wednesdays, College Football on Saturdays.
6
Customise Everything in Settings
Use the tabs above to change your city, voice type (female, male, girl, boy), tile colours and labels, and app preferences. Press Save Settings when done.
7
Go Back from Any Screen
The large ‹ back button at the top left of every screen always takes you back to the home screen.
8
Publish to Google Play Store
1. Make sure all 5 files are on GitHub (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png). 2. Go to pwabuilder.com and paste your GitHub Pages URL. 3. Click Package for Stores and choose Android. 4. Go to play.google.com/console and pay the $25 one-time fee. 5. Upload the .aab file, fill in your app description and screenshots, and submit. Google reviews in 1 to 7 days!
